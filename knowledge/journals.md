# Journal style tendencies

## Energy and Applied Energy

Favor direct engineering problem–solution logic, compact abstracts, quantitative validation, and practical relevance. Move quickly from the battery task to a specific operating/data bottleneck. Explain why each ML module is needed under realistic conditions. Generalization and deployment claims are stronger when supported across independent cells, chemistries, operating conditions, or system scales.

## Journal of Power Sources

Battery diagnostics, experimental protocols, state estimation, and electrochemical interpretation are central. AI descriptions are strongest when tied to health indicators, EIS, ICA/DVA, realistic operation, or interpretable cell behavior. Avoid implying mechanism from prediction accuracy alone.

## Energy Storage Materials

Materials/degradation context and mechanistic evidence carry greater weight. AI should accelerate discovery, reveal degradation science, or close an experimentally validated loop rather than appear as an isolated benchmark. For materials discovery, distinguish screening, prediction, candidate selection, experimental validation, and mechanistic follow-up.
